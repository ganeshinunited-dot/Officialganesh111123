After AdSense Approval

Ganesh Karki

I checked what happens after Google says yes, and three gates still stand between approval and your first payout.

First is tax information. Before Google can pay you, your payments profile needs tax details completed. For someone outside the United States that is generally a W-8BEN form, though you should confirm the exact form inside your own AdSense account rather than take my word for it. Payment can be held until this is done, so it is worth having ready.

Second is address verification. Once your earnings reach Google's verification threshold, Google mails a six-digit PIN to your payments address by ordinary post. There is no tracking number, and it usually takes about three weeks to arrive. You have four months from the date Google generates the PIN to enter it, and entering it wrong three times stops ads. It has to be an address that can actually receive standard post in Nepal, and the letter is one to watch for rather than assume lost after a week.

Third is the payment threshold. Your balance has to reach the threshold before Google issues a payment, and anything below it rolls over to the next month. Secondary guides put that threshold at typically 100 dollars, so confirm the figure for your account. Approval can show ads and still pay nothing for a while.

I also checked Growentix against this path, and two small gaps are worth fixing before the November 15 to 20 window. Your Privacy Policy currently says visitors get no cookies and no third-party sharing, which would stop being accurate the day ads go live. It needs an AdSense and cookies disclosure. There is no standalone contact page yet, and no ads.txt file. The contact page is a decision for before you apply, so a reviewer can find a way to reach you. The ads.txt file has to wait, because AdSense issues the publisher line for it, so I would queue it for right after approval.

None of this changes the plan you already have. It just means the November check should cover these gates too, and I will have the Privacy wording ready for it.
